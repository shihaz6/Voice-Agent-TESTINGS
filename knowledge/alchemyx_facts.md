## Products

### Catalyst — AI WhatsApp Front Desk
- What it is: An AI assistant that replies to customers on WhatsApp for the business, 24 hours a day.
- Built first for: Tutors and tuition classes. Also suitable for other businesses that get lots of customer messages, such as pharmacies and shops.
- Languages: Replies in English, Sinhala and Tamil, and understands Singlish and Tanglish.
- Human handover: When a question needs a real person, it hands the chat over to the owner.
- Payment tracking: Helps track student or customer payments.
- Comes with: A web dashboard for the owner.

### AI Voice Agent (this agent)
- What it is: An AI receptionist that answers phone calls for a business, answers common questions and takes callback requests.
- Status: Launching soon.
- Proof: The caller is talking to it right now.

### Custom AI projects
- Yes, we build custom AI projects. If a business has a repetitive task or a problem it wants automated with AI, the team can design and build a solution for it.
